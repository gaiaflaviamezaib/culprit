Demo recorder

Records your screen, a floating face bubble and your microphone. Nothing is uploaded: the video is saved to your Downloads folder.

1. Face bubble

Turns on your camera and microphone and opens your face in a small floating window that stays on top of everything. Drag it to a corner and resize it.

2. Record

Choose Entire screen in the window that opens, so the recording follows you between tabs and shows the face bubble. Then switch to the Culprit tab and present.

3. Your video

After you stop, the file downloads automatically. Upload it to YouTube as Public.
